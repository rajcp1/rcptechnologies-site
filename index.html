About Us

RCP Technologies Ltd delivers modular, audit-ready AWS automation frameworks for enterprise clients. Led by Rajesh, a senior cloud platform engineer with over 15 years of experience, we specialize in scalable infrastructure, secure cloud migrations, and compliance-driven DevOps solutions.

Our solutions have been deployed across diverse industries including retail, financial services, pharmaceuticals, and non-profit:

  • Financial Services: Led secure data centre migrations, Citrix virtualization, and enterprise infrastructure management across Active Directory, VMware, and Windows server estates — all aligned with regulatory compliance and operational continuity.
  • Pharmaceuticals: Directed cloud migration programs to AWS, including migration wave planning, 6 R analysis, and the setup of AWS Control Tower and landing zones to enforce governance and scalability across business units.
  • Non-Profit: Engineered AWS platforms with Lambda-based automation to streamline operations, reduce manual overhead, and deliver cost-effective cloud solutions tailored to mission-driven organizations.
